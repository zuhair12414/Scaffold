# Design

Not UI polish — the design decisions that determine whether the tutor helps or
harms.

## What belongs here
- `hint-taxonomy.md` — the levels, worked examples of each, and the rules for
  what each level may and may not reveal.
- `interaction.md` — when the tutor speaks, how the student asks, whether hints
  can be refused or rewound.
- `understanding-model.md` — the concept inventory and how belief is updated.
- Sketches, flows, state diagrams.

## Principle
Every design choice should be traceable to a claim about learning. If you
cannot say why a choice helps learning, it is a preference, and it should be
labelled as one.
