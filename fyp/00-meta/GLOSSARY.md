# Glossary

Keep terms here the moment they get used in two different senses.

- **Graded hint** — a hint at a defined level of the taxonomy in
  `ARCHITECTURE.md`. "Graded" means calibrated in strength, not marked/scored.
- **Understanding estimate** — the model's belief about what a student knows,
  per concept, with uncertainty attached.
- **Stuck episode** — a contiguous period of attempts on one task without
  meaningful progress. Hint escalation is scoped to an episode.
- **Productive struggle** — difficulty that produces learning. The thing the
  system exists to protect.
- **Over-help** — releasing a hint stronger than the student needed. The
  central failure mode.
- **Leak** — a hint that lets a student reconstruct the solution beyond what
  its level permits.
- **Transfer** — performance on a later, unaided task. The real outcome.
- **Trajectory** — the sequence of submissions and system responses for one
  student on one task.
