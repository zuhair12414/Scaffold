# Research

## Layout
- `papers/` — PDFs. Name them `year-firstauthor-shorttitle.pdf`.
- `notes/` — one note per paper, same stem as the PDF, `.md`.
- `lit-review/` — the synthesis that goes into the report.

## Areas to sweep
1. **Intelligent tutoring systems** — hint policies, model tracing, the
   classic Cognitive Tutor / ASSISTments literature.
2. **Knowledge tracing** — BKT, DKT, and the critiques of both.
3. **Hint design and help-seeking** — help abuse, gaming the system, the
   assistance dilemma (too much help and too little are both harmful).
4. **Productive failure / desirable difficulties** — the learning-science
   basis for withholding.
5. **LLMs in CS education** — what happens to learning when students have
   unrestricted access, and existing constrained-tutor attempts.
6. **Automated program analysis for feedback** — AST diffing, repair-based
   feedback, test-based localisation.

## The standard to hold
For every claim you plan to put in the report: what was measured, on whom, and
what would falsify it. A paper that asserts hints help without an unaided
post-test does not support our design.
